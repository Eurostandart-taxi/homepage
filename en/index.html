EuroStandart Taxi – Who We Are

EuroStandart Taxi has been operating in the Georgian market since 2019.
The company's primary activity is the leasing of light vehicles in the taxi sector.
In 2020 the company became a Yandex Go partner.
EuroStandart Taxi has an efficient and dynamic team that provides drivers with operational, technical and consultancy support.

Detailed Leasing Conditions
Leasing Conditions for One Driver
  • Lease term: 2 to 3 years
  • Driver's contribution: equivalent of $1,000 in GEL
    Can be spread over 3–6 months
  • Daily payment: 80 GEL
  • Insurance: 260 GEL per month
  • One day off per week
  • Minimum 260 working hours per month on Yandex
Leasing Conditions for Two Drivers
  • Lease term: 1.5 to 2 years
  • 2 drivers' contribution: equivalent of $1,000 in GEL
    Can be spread over 3–6 months
  • Daily payment for 2 drivers: 115 GEL
  • Insurance: 260 GEL per month
  • One day off per week
  • Minimum 460 working hours per month on Yandex
Operational Leasing Conditions for One Driver
  • Operational lease term: 3 years
  • Driver's income: 45% of earnings, bonus 100%
  • Daily payment
  • All fuel and vehicle operating costs covered by the company
  • Minimum 260 working hours per month on Yandex
  • After three years the driver will have the option to purchase the vehicle
